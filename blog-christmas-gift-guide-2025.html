🎅 Top Tips to Stay Active Over Christmas 🎅

We all know December can get busy, chaotic and full of temptations that pull us away from training. Here are three simple tips to keep you moving through the holidays:

1. Keep Sessions Short but Consistent
You don’t need an hour-long workout to stay on track. Even 15–20 minutes of movement (a quick EMOM, kettlebell circuit, or band workout) keeps momentum going.

2. Plan Activity Around Social Events
Walking to the shops, taking the kids to the park, or doing a quick session before heading out to a Christmas meal all count. Movement doesn’t have to be “gym training” to make a difference.

3. Don’t Aim for Perfection, Aim for Balance
Enjoy the food, enjoy the socialising, and enjoy the rest. Just keep a little movement in the mix and you’ll roll into January feeling far better than if you go completely off the rails.

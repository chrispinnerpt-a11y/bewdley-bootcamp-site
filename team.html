The coaches.

Experienced coaches who know how to get the best from you — whether it's your first ever class or your five-hundredth.

Founder & Head Coach

Chris Pinner

Chris has been coaching in Bewdley since 2010, when he founded Pinnacle Personal Training. What started as a one-to-one PT business grew into Bewdley Bootcamp — the town's only dedicated functional fitness gym, on the edge of the Wyre Forest.

He designs every week of class programming — strength days, conditioning and mobility — and still coaches on the floor, alongside one-to-one personal training clients. His approach: structured, progressive training that fits real life, in a gym where everyone's welcome.

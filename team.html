Meet the team

The coaches.

Experienced coaches who know how to get the best from you, whether it's your first ever class or your five-hundredth.

Founder & Head Coach

Chris Pinner

Chris has been a personal trainer since graduating from Durham University with a degree in Sport and Exercise Science in 2009, and opening his own gym had been a dream since then.

Bewdley Bootcamp itself started life as weekly outdoor sessions on a muddy field in Bewdley through COVID, before Chris finally secured the unit on Bewdley Business Park in January 2022. He writes every week of class programming and still coaches on the floor, alongside his one-to-one personal training clients.

Everybody starts somewhere. It's just about showing up. It's a genuinely friendly space, everything gets adapted to your abilities, and you go at your own pace. The community in the gym is everything, everyone ends up being everybody else's biggest cheerleader.

What Chris wants every new member to know
Favourite exercise to programme
Burpees.
One he dreads
Anything overhead with a barbell, thanks to old rugby shoulder injuries.
Outside the gym
Chris is dad to two girls, Sofia and Lily, and married to Niki, aka Mrs Bootcamp. He also runs a monthly run club with Rise Cafe, and still gets back on the rugby pitch whenever he can after years captaining Kidderminster Carolians.
Train with Chris one-to-one

Coach & Personal Trainer

Liz

Our resident former veterinary nurse turned barbell enthusiast. Liz spent years looking after the animals before swapping the veterinary surgery for the squat rack.

She started coaching group fitness back in 2019, then went all in, completing her Level 2 Gym Instructor and Level 3 Personal Trainer qualifications, followed by CrossFit Level 2 and British Weightlifting Level 1.

I remember exactly how it feels to be in your shoes, and I will always make you feel welcome and meet you where you're at.

What Liz wants every new member to know
Favourite exercise to programme
Anything with a barbell or on the rig.
One she dreads teaching
Kettlebell cleans. Pretty technical for such an innocent looking exercise, and they can smart on the wrist if you get it wrong. Liz has you covered on technique though.
What gets her most excited
Proving fitness isn't just about cardio, and watching someone land their first pull-up or kick up to handstand after they never believed they could.
Outside the gym
Training with her husband and son, or being dragged out for another walk by Dora the cocker spaniel. Ask her about her latest home interiors project, she won't need much encouragement!
Find her
Coaching the Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 9.30am classes, and available for 1-1 personal training throughout the week.

Coach & Personal Trainer

Tiff

13 years deep into personal training and still just as obsessed with it as day one. Tiff studied Psychology at university, and has always loved everything fitness.

You can always push so much harder than you believe. I will help you get there, no matter where your starting point is!

What Tiff wants every new member to know
Favourite exercises to programme
Burpees and deadlifts.
One she dislikes
Back squats. Yes, even the coaches have exercises they're not fussed about.
What gets her most excited
Watching someone smash a tough goal, a first pull-up or a rope climb especially. She's passionate about working with pregnant and postnatal clients, supporting them both physically and mentally through that stage.
Outside the gym
Training herself, off on campervan trips with her partner Bod and their two children, Fox and Juno, or renovating a house. Yes, that's right, renovating with two toddlers underfoot. She is a brave woman. She's also just picked football back up after a 13 year break.
Find her
Teaching our regular bootcamp classes on Wednesdays at 12:30 and Thursdays at 17:45.

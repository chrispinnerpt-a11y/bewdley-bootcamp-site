Classes

Functional fitness,
properly programmed.

Bewdley Bootcamp classes are a mix of strength training, bodyweight work and conditioning circuits — coached, scalable, and built as one structured weekly programme. Suitable for all abilities.

The programme

What a week looks like.

Rather than random workouts, every week balances the three pillars. Miss a day and you can pick the thread back up — the programme is designed so any combination of classes still adds up.

Strength days

Barbell-led sessions built around the big three — squat, bench and deadlift — with percentage-based loading so you progress at your own numbers. Log your lifts in Bootcamp Track and the app calculates your working weights for you.

Conditioning

High-intensity circuits, partner challenges and engine-building intervals. Constantly varied so it never gets stale — and every station scales to your level on the day.

Mobility

Dedicated work on how you move — full ranges, better positions, faster recovery. The pillar most gyms skip, and the one that keeps you training for the long haul.

Who it's for

All abilities. Genuinely.

Every class is coached and every movement has an easier and harder version. You'll never be the odd one out — beginners train next to experienced lifters, each working at their own level.

  • Every exercise modified to your needs
  • Experienced coaches on the floor every session
  • A welcoming crowd — no egos, no mirrors-and-selfies culture
  • Surrounded by the Wyre Forest, not a retail park

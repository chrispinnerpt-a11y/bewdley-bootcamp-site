Personal Training

One-to-one coaching.

Classes are the heart of the gym — personal training is the accelerator. Individual programming, technique coaching and accountability with Chris Pinner, delivering results in Bewdley since 2010.

How it works

It starts with a free consultation.

Everything begins with a free one-hour consultation — no poking and prodding, just a proper conversation about what you want to achieve, why, and how we'll get there. Together we'll decide the best way to track progress: the scales, body composition, performance goals, or simply how your clothes fit and feel.

From day one you'll have focused strategies built around your life — including an analysis of your current nutrition and a sustainable plan to improve it.

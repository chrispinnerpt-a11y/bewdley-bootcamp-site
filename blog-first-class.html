Your first Bootcamp class: exactly what to expect

The hardest rep of any training journey is walking through the door for the first time. So here's exactly what happens when you do — no surprises, no mystery.

Before you arrive

Book your class online — a drop-in is £8. When you sign up you'll be emailed a short medical form; please fill it in before your first session. Then all you need is comfortable kit, trainers and a water bottle. Everything else is provided.

When you walk in

Come five or ten minutes early and introduce yourself to the coach — we'll be expecting you. The gym is a friendly, small-group environment: no intimidating weights-room culture, no one filming themselves in the mirror. Just people training.

The session itself

  1. Warm-up — coached as a group, preparing exactly the movements you're about to do.
  2. The main work — strength, conditioning or mobility depending on the day. The coach demonstrates everything, then keeps an eye on you throughout.
  3. Scaling — every single exercise has an easier and a harder version. Tell the coach it's your first class and you'll get the right one. Nobody expects you to keep up with people who've trained for years — that's not the point.
  4. Warm-down — and usually a bit of a laugh. Classes finish on time, every time.

Afterwards

Expect to feel it a bit the next day — that's normal and it fades fast as your body adapts. The best thing you can do is book your second class before the soreness talks you out of it. Consistency beats intensity, every time.

You don't need to get fit before you come. Getting fit is what the classes are for.
